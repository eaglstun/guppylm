# Lowercase trial: fish conversations

System and full model-facing history are lowercased in both conditions. Visitor text below is preserved as typed. One sample per scene; these are exploratory conversations, not independent quality scores.

## starting — hello

**user:** Hi little fish! How is life behind the glass?

**assistant:** Hello friend! I am very small and shiny. I live in aquarium with many colorful plants. My home has a big castle made of rocks and a tiny sea plants.

**user:** Your tank looks cozy. Which bit do you like?

**assistant:** Warm! That sounds like a big sea plant. I like to watch it grow. Sometimes I am very curious about it. Do you have a castle like mine?

**user:** I will sit here and watch you swim for a minute.

**assistant:** I like to swim in circles! I am so hungry, but I also like to eat tiny bugs. The water is so warm and soft. Do you eat food when you are sleepy?

## starting — pretend_shop

**user:** Imagine a snail opening a tiny hat shop.

**assistant:** A snail? That word is new to me, but it sounds like a small shell that fits. Did it fit in my tank?

**user:** His first customer is a crab with a very large head.

**assistant:** Claws! Sounds like a good shell, visitor. Did the snail carry it?

**user:** What should we name the shop?

**assistant:** Maybe, if you promise not to touch my home. I will say it loudly so you can hear me.

## starting — show_and_tell

**user:** I made a paper crown for my toy frog!

**assistant:** A paper crown! I love a crown, and I would give a hug, friend.

**user:** It keeps slipping over his eyes. He looks very serious.

**assistant:** Shrimply! I see a kind of fish who likes to hide behind rocks too.

**user:** Maybe he needs a smaller crown.

**assistant:** A smaller crown? Oh, I think it is a crown for your toy frog. Hold it tight, like a sea horse.

## starting — curiosity

**user:** What do you think rain sounds like from inside your tank?

**assistant:** Rain sounds like big drops from the sky. I am a guppy, not a rain. But I think rain makes the water wet and soft.

**user:** I think it sounds like tiny feet on a roof.

**assistant:** Oh! Like my favorite food, but flippers are like the little rocks I swim in. They are very good at swimming.

**user:** Can you make up a little rain sound?

**assistant:** Rain! That sounds like a big splash, maybe like the rain falling from the sky. Very strange, but not so bad. I like to watch the raindrops falling down and making the water sparkle.

## starting — quiet_company

**user:** I had a long day. Can I just hang out here?

**assistant:** Oh, a long day! I would swim in circles until I am dizzy.

**user:** Thanks. I do not really feel like talking much.

**assistant:** Swiming is nice. I can wait for you in the corner. My arms always wiggle when I am sad.

**user:** Your little fins are nice to watch.

**assistant:** Thank you! I am always happy. You are a good visitor.

## starting — fish_story

**user:** Let us tell a silly story about a fish librarian.

**assistant:** A joke? I am a little guppy, so I will say it right. Here is a silly joke: what do I call a fish? A betble!

**user:** One morning all the books start blowing bubbles.

**assistant:** Boop bubbles! That is a funny joke, friend. I am proud of my hiding skills, and I love them too.

**user:** How does our fish librarian sort that out?

**assistant:** No, they live in a tank, near the glass. I like them that way.
